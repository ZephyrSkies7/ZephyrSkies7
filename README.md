Hi. I'm Zephyr.

I'm a 3D artist who excels at hard-surface modelling with competency in both low-poly and high-poly Sub-D workflows, with a specialisation in designing and texturing low-poly models for sci-fi projects. I have integration and scripting experience related to animation/behaviour for 3D models inside CPP-related frameworks, and further have working knowledge using Lua code for both game logic and front-end development, with some exposure to the RmlUi HTML/CSS UI library and migration efforts surrounding it.

I began 3D Modelling in 2024 for the open-source Beyond All Reason RTS project, and quickly pivoted from starting as a junior 3D modeller, to senior 3D modeller, to then one of the lead 3D designers for the game's Legion faction. 

In 2025, under my supervision, Beyond All Reason's art department was restructured completely to introduce a clear hierarchy of the artists and developers on the team, ensuring that communication, development, and feedback lifecycles would be significantly smoother. Within this effort, I was assigned a Project and Directing Manager for the department, where I oversaw and collaborated with multiple leads across the 3D Art, Sound Design, Environment Art, UI/UX, and Campaign Development/Writing teams, coordinating the work of over 26 artists and developers.

Due to my efforts:
  * Lead times for sub-project completions were, on average, cut in half or more, the accuracy of appropriate assignment of contributors on the team to sub-projects had a significant uptick. 
  * Contributors within the art department, both new and old, felt more aligned with project directives and goals on average, and often expressed satisfaction that both they and their work was receiving more visbility from the playerbase and upper management alike.
  * Multiple projects previously left on ice were revived and procured regular development updates, and projects not on ice had their development rates skyrocket, including but not limited to:
    * The Legion Faction: Rate of development on the 3D design side skyrocketed with over 150 under my guidance alone, and the sub-project became one of the few, if not the very first in BAR's history, to receive a deadline and complete it fully, weeks ahead of said deadline.
    * Faction Design Overhaul: Long-outdated designs for existing factions were slated for a refresh, and under my management this project came very close to ending the concept and design phase and beginning implementation and delivery efforts.
    * RmlUi Integration: A long-standing goal of the UI/UX team to update the aging and slow Lua-based user interface to a C++ library based on HTML/CSS was revived and got startlingly close to full completion.
    * New Lobby/Main Menu/Interface Development: Related to the effort listed above, I spearheaded and managed the development timeline for the still in-progress massive UI/UX that would touch upon all aspects of the game, from boot-up to game close. 
    * Campaign/Lore Development: Working closely with lore lead Volshok, the project was revived and was finally delivered to completion in 2026 after years of discussion-based quagmires.

During this time, I also served as an advisor to Zecrus, the Balance Lead for BAR, regarding game balance and logic specifically surrounding Legion, as well as other aspects of the game later on such as inter-tier progression and naval combat/interaction. Additionally, an in-progress collaboration with the TMC team, renowned for their beloved Hired Steel cinematics and official MechWarrior 5 VFX/cinematics, was brokered under my watch during this time as well.

in 2026, I parted ways with the BAR team for a new challenge, and began working as a 3D designer with Splitscreen Games on their award-winning sci-fi MMORPG, Pirate Galaxy, where I continue to develop my skills.

For my professional career, I work for a major retailer as a senior business analyst and data engineer.
